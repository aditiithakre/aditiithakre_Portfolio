About

I'm a computer science student at IIIT Pune, graduating in 2028. I'm interested in artificial intelligence alongside frontend development and UI/UX — most of what I know, I've learned by building.

Inventory Management System

Full-stack stock tracking with barcode scanning

Built with a team of four. The tool splits by role: admins get stock oversight and reporting dashboards, staff get a camera barcode scanner that updates quantities on the spot, with manual entry when the camera won't cooperate. Every action lands in an inventory log, and low stock triggers an email alert. This was my first hackathon and the first real thing I'd built with a team — I contributed on the frontend, building out the React interface and learning most of it as I went.

Personal project

FairShare

Expense splitter for hostels and flats

A command-line expense splitter for students and roommates — food, rent, utilities, trips — that works out who owes whom and recommends the fewest payments to settle up. Written in Python with no dependencies beyond the standard library. Two decisions I'd defend: money is stored as integer paise so nothing drifts, and balances are recomputed from the raw expense history rather than kept as running state. A web version is next.
